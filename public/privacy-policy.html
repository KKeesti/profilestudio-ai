Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Who is processing your data?

Your data is processed by LihtneAI OÜ.
Contact email: oleg@lihtneai.ee

2. What data we collect and why

We collect and process the following data specifically for the operation of our SaaS AI Portrait Generator:

  • Photos of your face: We collect the images you upload solely for the purpose of generating your AI portrait.
  • Email address: Used to save your generated results, give you access to your generation history, handle support requests, and link your payments/credits to your account.
  • Generation history: We save your generated images so you can access them later.
  • Payment data: Processed securely by our third-party provider (Stripe). We do not store your credit card details, only the status of your payment and purchased credits.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic device information to prevent abuse and spam.

3. Where we process your data

Your data is processed on our secure hosting and database servers (Supabase/Render) and through trusted cloud services. The actual AI generation is performed via the Google Gemini API. Minimal data required for generation is temporarily transmitted securely to Google for processing.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Original Photos: Not retained longer than necessary for generation. Original image payloads may be discarded shortly after generation to protect your privacy.
  • Generated Images & Email: Retained in your account history so you can download them, until you request deletion.

5. Your Rights & How to delete your data

You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data.

  • You can request the complete deletion of your account, all uploaded photos, and generated images at any time.
  • To delete your data or revoke your consent, please email us at oleg@lihtneai.ee with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

6. International Data Transfers

If our third-party infrastructure providers operate outside the relevant European Economic Area (EEA), your data may be transferred internationally. We ensure all providers comply with GDPR-equivalent safeguards (e.g., standard contractual clauses).
