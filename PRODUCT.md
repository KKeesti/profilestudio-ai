# ShotMe.ee Product Direction

## Primary user

A person with one important damaged family photograph who wants a useful result without learning an AI tool.

## First-value moment

The visitor uploads an old photo, receives a restored and colorized result, compares it with the original, and downloads it.

## Restore entry point

- `/restore` is the focused landing flow used by old-photo advertising.
- The first viewport contains the promise, upload action, free-use terms, and identity-safety reassurance.
- Uploading starts `RESTORE_OLD_PHOTO` immediately; there is no style-selection step.
- The original remains available for comparison and is never overwritten in the interface.
- Do not claim a provider retention period or no-training policy until it is contractually verified.

## Studio entry point

- `/` remains the broader portrait studio.
- Free users choose a preset and generation starts immediately.
- Manual text and voice refinements remain paid features.

## Measurement

Measure CTA visibility, upload clicks, selected files, successful generations, downloads, device, language, source, campaign, and `utm_content` for each video.
